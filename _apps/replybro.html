Privacy Policy

ReplyBro 3.0  ·  Last updated: November 4, 2026

Overview

ReplyBro ("we", "our", "the app") answers chat messages for you on Android. It reads the notifications of the messaging apps you choose and replies through each app's own Reply button, using your rules, your status, and optionally AI. This policy explains what the app reads, what stays on your phone, and what leaves it.

Short version: ReplyBro has no server and no accounts. Your chats, rules and history stay on your phone. Messages leave it only if you turn on AI replies and agree: then the chat being answered goes directly from your phone to the AI provider you set up, with your own key. Free users see Google ads only when they choose to watch one.

1. What ReplyBro Reads

With the notification access you grant in Android Settings, ReplyBro reads new notifications from the messaging apps you turn on in the app (WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business by default; Telegram, Instagram, Messenger, Signal, Messages, Slack, Teams and Discord if you add them). From each chat notification it uses:

  • the sender's name and the chat or group name, as the messaging app shows them;
  • the new message and the recent messages that the notification includes;
  • the notification's Reply button, to send your reply.

Other notifications are ignored. Notifications that look like one-time codes, payments, ID requests or emergencies are never answered automatically (the "Safety guard", on by default), and notifications whose content Android or the app hides are skipped.

2. What Stays on Your Phone

ReplyBro stores the following only on your device:

  • Rules your quick-reply rules and per-chat choices (for example "never reply to this chat").
  • Activity a history of messages ReplyBro handled and the replies it sent or drafted. It is deleted automatically after 30 days, and you can clear it any time in Settings.
  • Settings your status, schedule, signature, apps to handle and other preferences.
  • Today's plan the times and statuses of your day, with your own notes. If you import your calendar, event titles are kept as private notes.
  • Brain what you tell the AI about yourself: name, description, tone, facts it may share, and things it must never do.
  • API key your AI provider key, encrypted with the Android Keystore.
  • Free plan your diamond balance, daily streak, ads watched today, and today's count of AI replies.

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include your settings, today's plan, Brain profile and diamond balance in your Google account backup. Rules, activity history and your API key are never backed up.

3. AI Replies (Optional, Off by Default)

AI replies stay off until you turn them on in the app and agree to a notice that explains what is sent. When a message has no matching rule, ReplyBro then sends the AI provider:

  • up to the 12 most recent messages of that chat, with the senders' names, and the chat or group name;
  • your Brain profile (name, description, tone, facts you allow it to share, and your "never" list);
  • your current status (for example "in a meeting until 2 PM");
  • only for contacts you choose to share your day with: the rest of today's plan as times and free/busy. Your notes and calendar event titles are never sent.

Messages caught by the Safety guard are not sent. The sample you type in the Brain screen's Try it box is sent the same way when you tap it.

Where it goes: directly from your phone to the provider you set up, using your own API key: Google Gemini by default, or an OpenAI-compatible service you configure. We never receive these messages. The provider handles them under its own terms:

  • Google Gemini: with a free (unpaid) Gemini API key, Google may use what is sent and the replies to improve its products, and human reviewers may read them, disconnected from your account (Gemini API terms). With a paid key, or if you are in the EEA, Switzerland or the UK, Google does not use them to improve its products. Google requires API users to be 18 or older.
  • Other providers: see the privacy terms of the service you configure.

You can turn AI replies off at any time in the Brain screen; nothing more is sent after that.

4. Calendar and Voice Input (Optional)

Calendar: when you tap From calendar while planning your day, ReplyBro asks for calendar access and reads the start, end, title and busy/free status of today's remaining events. It reads them on your phone to build your plan; titles stay on your phone as private notes.

Voice: when you tap the microphone to speak your plan, your phone's speech recognition service (for example Google's) turns your speech into text under that service's privacy policy. ReplyBro only receives the text.

5. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform (free plan only). Free users can watch rewarded ads to earn diamonds. While a free user has the app open, ReplyBro loads an ad in the background and shows it only when they tap Watch ad. AdMob may collect device identifiers such as the Advertising ID, your IP address, and ad interaction data, and may personalise ads where you allow it. Where the law requires (for example in the EEA and UK), Google's consent form appears before any ad is requested, and Settings → Ad privacy choices lets you change your choice. Pro subscribers see no ads and the ad SDK is not started.

Firebase Crashlytics (Google). If the app crashes, a crash report is sent so we can fix it: the error and stack trace, device model, Android version, app version and a random installation ID. Crash reports never include messages, contact names, replies or your API key.

Reporting an AI reply. In Activity you can report a reply the AI wrote (for example, if it was offensive or wrong). The report is sent to us through Firebase Crashlytics with the next upload: your reason, any note you type, the AI provider and the messaging app. The message and the reply are included only if you tick Include this message and reply. We use reports only to improve AI replies.

Google Play Billing. Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details; the app only learns whether you have an active subscription.

6. Replies Sent in Your Name

ReplyBro sends replies from your own messaging accounts, so the people you chat with see them as coming from you. Replies on the free plan end with "— Auto-reply by ReplyBro". You decide which apps, chats and messages ReplyBro may answer, and you are responsible for the replies it sends for you.

7. Permissions Explained

  • Notification access: required to read chat notifications and reply to them. You can revoke it any time in Android Settings.
  • Notifications: to show drafts to approve, messages that need you, and notices such as "out of diamonds".
  • Ignore battery optimisation (optional): so Android doesn't stop ReplyBro in the background.
  • Do Not Disturb access: to know when your phone is in Do Not Disturb, if you schedule replies for that time.
  • Calendar (optional): only to import today's events into your plan, as described above.
  • Internet: for AI replies you turn on, ads on the free plan, crash reports, and Google Play.
  • Billing: for Pro subscriptions through Google Play.
  • Advertising ID (added by the AdMob SDK): used by Google for ads on the free plan.

8. Sharing and Selling

We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we have no server that receives it. Data leaves your phone only as described above: chats to the AI provider you set up (if AI is on), ad data to Google AdMob (free plan), crash reports and any AI-reply reports you send to Firebase Crashlytics, and purchases through Google Play.

9. Retention and Deletion

  • Activity history is deleted automatically after 30 days; Settings → Clear activity history deletes it at once.
  • Uninstalling ReplyBro deletes everything it stores on your phone. A Google account backup, if any, follows Android's backup settings.
  • Messages sent to your AI provider are kept according to that provider's terms.
  • Crashlytics keeps crash reports and AI-reply reports for up to 90 days. AdMob data is kept under Google's policies.

10. Security

Your API key is encrypted with the Android Keystore and never leaves your phone except to your AI provider. All network connections use HTTPS.

11. Children's Privacy

ReplyBro is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. AI replies need an API key from a provider whose own age rules apply (Google requires users to be 18 or older).

12. Changes to This Policy

We will update this page when the app's data practices change, and change the date at the top. If we add a service that receives your messages, such as a ReplyBro AI server, this policy will describe it before the app uses it.
