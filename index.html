// 01.00 About

Background & Philosophy

SEC. ABOUT INDEX [01/04]

I'm Shubham, an independent developer shipping Android apps under ishubhamx and SSV Makers. I like small products with one clear job: a calendar you open every morning, a scanner that works offline, a puzzle you play in the queue, an assistant that answers chats while you're busy.

Most of my work is native Android in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, with games in Godot and web and desktop tools in TypeScript. I design, build, test and publish each app myself, privacy policy included.

01

One job, done well

No bloat and no feature soup. Each app solves one problem and gets out of your way.

02

Privacy in plain words

Every app has a short, readable policy that says exactly what stays on your device. Read them all.

03

Details that feel good

Smooth motion, thoughtful defaults and dark mode. The little things are the product.
