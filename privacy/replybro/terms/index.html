2. What ReplyBro Does

ReplyBro answers chat messages for you on Android. With the notification access you grant, it reads new notifications from the messaging apps you turn on (WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business by default; Telegram, Instagram, Messenger, Signal, Messages, Slack, Teams and Discord on Pro) and replies through each app's own Reply button, using your rules, your status, your plan for the day and, if you turn it on, AI.

ReplyBro has no server and no accounts. Rules, history and settings stay on your phone. Messages leave your phone only if you turn on AI replies, as described in section 4 and in the Privacy Policy.
