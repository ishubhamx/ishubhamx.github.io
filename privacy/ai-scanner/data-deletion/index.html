Privacy-First Architecture: Scan to PDF is an account-free, on-device utility. We do not require accounts, logins, or cloud synchronization. Your documents, photos, signatures, OCR texts, and object counts reside exclusively on your physical device.

1. Instant On-Device Data Deletion (User Self-Service)

Because your scanned files and preferences never leave your device, you have complete and immediate control to delete them at any time:

  1. Delete Individual Scans: Open the app, swipe left or tap the trash icon on any document or folder to permanently erase it from device storage.
  2. Clear All App Data via Android Settings:
    • Open your phone's Settings.
    • Navigate to Apps (or App Management) > Scan to PDF.
    • Tap Storage & cache.
    • Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data). This immediately wipes all local database records, cached thumbnails, and saved documents.
  3. Uninstall the Application: Uninstalling Scan to PDF permanently purges all sandboxed application files, temporary caches, and database tables from your phone.

2. Deletion of Telemetry & Diagnostic Data (Firebase & AdMob)

The app integrates trusted Google SDKs for stability and ads:

  • Firebase Crashlytics: Anonymized crash logs and device hardware details.
  • Firebase Analytics: Aggregated screen navigation metrics (no personal document contents or OCR data).
  • Google AdMob: Standard pseudonymous advertising identifiers (GAID).

If you wish to submit a formal request to purge any associated pseudonymous telemetry identifiers from our analytics dashboards, you may submit a request directly to our developer team.

3. How to Submit a Data Deletion Request

To request data deletion, email our developer support team with the following details:

  • Subject: Data Deletion Request - Scan to PDF
  • Your Google Advertising ID (GAID) or device model (optional, to help locate anonymized crash/analytics traces).
  • Send email to: ssvmakers@gmail.com

Request Data Deletion via Email

4. Response Timeline & Retention

  • Local Files: Deleted immediately by you via device settings or uninstallation.
  • Developer Request Processing: We acknowledge and process all data deletion email requests within 30 days.
  • Retention: We do not operate remote document servers or retain user documents after local deletion.
