AMZO privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What AMZO collects

Nothing. AMZO has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising SDKs. It does not ask for your name, phone number, email, location, or store logins.

Data stored on your phone

The sales you set reminders for, and the latest copy of the deals list, are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Network requests

AMZO downloads its list of deals and sales from GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub may log the IP address of that request; see the GitHub privacy statement. Product images are loaded from the stores' own servers.

Links to stores

Deals and sales open on the store's website or app, such as Amazon or Flipkart. What you do there is covered by that store's privacy policy. Some links are affiliate links, which means AMZO may earn a commission on purchases; this does not change the price you pay.

Notifications

AMZO asks for notification permission only to show the sale reminders you turn on.
